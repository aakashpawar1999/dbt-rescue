# DBT Rescue visual system

The landing page tells the product story; `/demo` is the dedicated working experience. A family member explores the product at home, then a citizen or helper reads the action plan on a phone in daylight. Both surfaces use the existing forest-green, copper and system-font identity. The landing hero commits to forest green; the workspace uses bright, calm surfaces with restrained semantic colour.

## Structure

- Landing: product navigation, clear promise, representative synthetic case, ordered workflow, real fixture inventory, trust and limitations, native FAQ, demo entry and evidence links. No adoption statistics, fabricated testimonials or official branding.
- Demo: product return, language/helper/reset controls, compact safety notice, four starts. A selected case shows identity, confirmed stage, reason, owner, next action and follow-up together; evidence and source provenance expand below it. Packet, acknowledgement and tracker remain separate working states.
- Maintain the native `/demo` URL on direct entry and refresh; asset requests use the canonical root internally without redirecting visitors away from the demo.

## Tokens and components

Use the existing 4px spacing scale and system sans stack. Landing display type is fluid, capped below 6rem; task headings use stable rem sizes. Heading tracking stays at or above -0.04em. White/light surfaces use dark forest text; copper indicates attention, green indicates primary actions or confirmed states. Every state also has a text label.

Buttons and links retain visible focus and at least 44px primary targets. Native selects, buttons and details provide intake, language choices, disclosures and FAQ behaviour. Use 6–12px radii and avoid decorative shadows, gradients and card grids. Semantic background and border changes convey hover and selection; reduced motion removes transitions.

## Content and state

Never translate by fixture position: retain event identity and selected-rule identity. Missing credit evidence selects the unknown fallback in both languages. Known-status choices name the supported synthetic scheme and route; they do not manufacture a case or payment confirmation. Unsupported cases keep an honest fallback. Dates in the existing fixture evidence remain historical synthetic dates.

All changed Hindi guidance and official-access wording require human review before release. The current follow-up wording requests an acknowledged date from the responsible office and claims no official SLA. Later synthetic deadline demonstrations must remain explicitly distinguished from official service promises.

## Verification

Verify both demo languages at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1280px. Check first-fold action visibility at 360×800 and 1280×720, keyboard and focus order, zoom, reduced motion, print, direct links and browser Back/forward after reset. Screenshots and interaction evidence complement domain tests; static markup does not establish accessibility compliance.

The landing and demo are release candidates until the full v1.0.0 contract, human reviews and deployment checks pass. This document does not certify a security or accessibility audit.
