# dbt-rescue 0.6.2

## Delivered

0.6.2 fixes three visible spacing defects in the existing DBT Rescue
interface. Above 1000px, the stepper shows all seven compact captions inside the
existing content width; at tablet and phone widths it keeps all numbered states
visible and shows one word-safe active caption. The English fourth-step caption
is `Fix the issue`, balancing its adjacent desktop captions without a unique
font size. Correction-packet values remain
in a right-hand column, including the fictional account value. The prototype
warning renders its two safety sentences as one continuous body text item
without an artificial flex-item gap. The Vite Worker now explicitly receives
the `ASSETS` binding it already expects, removing the local runtime overlay.
`Start over` now reads as a proper secondary button while retaining its 44px
target and keyboard focus treatment.

The release preserves all three fictional cases, English and Hindi, assisted
mode, browser history, step positioning, reset behavior, invalid-reference
handling, printable packets, acknowledgement, and simulated recovery updates.
It adds no runtime dependency.

## Boundary

All cases, payment events, acknowledgements, corrections, reprocessing, and
credit states remain synthetic or simulated. DBT Rescue does not connect to
live government or banking APIs, process real citizen data, determine
eligibility, correct real records, release money, or confirm a real credit.
