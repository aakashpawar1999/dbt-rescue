# dbt-rescue 0.6.2

## Delivered

0.6.2 fixes three visible spacing defects in the existing DBT Rescue
interface. On narrow screens, every active progress label stays within the
viewport and wraps only between words. Correction-packet values remain in a
right-hand column, including the fictional account value. The prototype warning
renders its two safety sentences as one continuous body text item without an
artificial flex-item gap.

The release preserves all three fictional cases, English and Hindi, assisted
mode, browser history, step positioning, reset behavior, invalid-reference
handling, printable packets, acknowledgement, and simulated recovery updates.
It adds no runtime dependency.

## Boundary

All cases, payment events, acknowledgements, corrections, reprocessing, and
credit states remain synthetic or simulated. DBT Rescue does not connect to
live government or banking APIs, process real citizen data, determine
eligibility, correct real records, release money, or confirm a real credit.
