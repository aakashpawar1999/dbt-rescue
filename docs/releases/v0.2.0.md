# dbt-rescue 0.2.0

## Outcome

A citizen selects one of three fictional payment references, understands the
route and latest confirmed event, receives the reviewed owner and next action,
and avoids a grievance when the payment reached another mapped account.

## Included

- `DBT-SUNITA-001`: Aadhaar-based farmer-benefit payment credited to the newer mapped Bank B.
- `DBT-ARJUN-002`: account-based scholarship payment returned for an invalid IFSC in the scheme record.
- `DBT-MEENA-003`: Aadhaar-based pension payment stopped because no active DBT bank mapping was available.
- Source-labelled absolute timestamps, missing and conflicting event visibility, and deterministic rules.
- Scenario-specific trace or correction packets using fictional and masked values.
- Bun-managed install, scripts, lockfile, CI, and release checks.

## Limits

All scheme, PFMS, NPCI, bank, Aadhaar, authentication, grievance, acknowledgement,
reprocessing, and credit behavior is fictional or simulated. No live service,
real personal information, or real payment movement is involved.
