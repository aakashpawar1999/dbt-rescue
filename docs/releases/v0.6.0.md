# dbt-rescue 0.6.0

## Delivered

0.6.0 improves the public DBT Rescue prototype's discoverability and visual
clarity without changing its deterministic, fictional-data safety boundary.
It adds complete static metadata, local favicon and manifest assets, a branded
social preview, a visible logo lockup, and a shared 4px spacing scale across
the seven-step English and Hindi recovery interface.

It also keeps a persistent English/Hindi footer that identifies the hackathon
project, states that it is not a government service, and repeats the fictional
data and no-real-credentials warning.

The social-preview banner uses the supplied glow logo and the final orange
`#B54E0B` / green `#004A35` palette.

The release keeps the active progress state visible on narrow screens, keeps
interactive targets usable, preserves the printable trace/correction packet,
and retains the existing Sunita, Arjun, and Meena behaviours.

## Boundary

All cases, payment events, acknowledgements, corrections, reprocessing, and
credit states remain synthetic or simulated. DBT Rescue does not connect to
live government or banking APIs, process real citizen data, determine
eligibility, correct real records, release money, or confirm a real credit.
