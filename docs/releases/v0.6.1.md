# dbt-rescue 0.6.1

## Delivered

0.6.1 fixes the public DBT Rescue journey's responsive positioning without
changing its fictional-data safety boundary or deterministic recovery content.
After a step transition, the existing progress tracker stays visible with the
active step heading and card content positioned below it. The long active
`Acknowledgement` label now wraps within the progress cell at 320px, and reset
returns the user to the branded start screen at the top of the document.

The release preserves all three fictional cases, English and Hindi, assisted
mode, browser history, invalid-reference handling, printable packets,
acknowledgement, and simulated recovery updates.

## Boundary

All cases, payment events, acknowledgements, corrections, reprocessing, and
credit states remain synthetic or simulated. DBT Rescue does not connect to
live government or banking APIs, process real citizen data, determine
eligibility, correct real records, release money, or confirm a real credit.
