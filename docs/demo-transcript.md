# DBT Rescue demo transcript

Sunita is expecting a farmer-benefit payment. A status can say money reached a
mapped bank while the account she checks is different. The hard part is knowing
which organisation can resolve the mismatch.

PFMS, NPCI, banks, and scheme systems already provide separate pieces. DBT
Rescue connects the evidence, owner, next action, and follow-up around one
recovery outcome.

This is a safe local fixture, not a live lookup. No Aadhaar, bank number, OTP,
or login is needed.

The timeline keeps each source and timestamp visible. It shows the newer mapped
Bank B credit and the older Bank A check as a conflict instead of inventing a
missing payment.

The reviewed rule says to check Bank B first. The audit view exposes the
fictional event, reason, source, route, review date, and rule status.

DBT Rescue gives the responsible trace desk and a masked, printable request. It
does not file a complaint or move money.

This acknowledgement is simulated. A real service would receive its
confirmation from the responsible organisation.

The local tracker records a simulated resolution state while keeping the
boundary clear: this is not a bank confirmation.

The build is functional for three synthetic cases; integrations, eligibility,
correction, and payment movement remain outside the prototype. Codex
contributed to research translation, implementation, testing, accessibility,
debugging, and documentation.
